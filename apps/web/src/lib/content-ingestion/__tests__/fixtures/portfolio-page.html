Rowan Ellis

Rowan Ellis is a product designer at Studio Marrow, where they spend most of their week on clinical software: the scheduling tools, consent flows, and results dashboards that hospital staff use every single day without ever choosing to.

Before Studio Marrow, Rowan spent six years inside a hospital group's own product team, which is where the interest in systems that survive a night shift began. They still keep a folder of photographs of laminated workarounds taped to monitors.
