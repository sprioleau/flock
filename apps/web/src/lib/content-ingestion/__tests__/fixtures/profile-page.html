Amara Osei

Professor of Environmental Engineering

Amara Osei studies urban heat islands and the design of shaded public space in fast-growing cities, with a focus on the neighborhoods that warm fastest.

She directs the Riverside Urban Climate Lab, where a team of twelve researchers maps street-level temperature across the metro area each summer.

Before joining the faculty in 2018, she spent six years as a design engineer on municipal infrastructure projects in Accra and Lisbon.

Her work has been supported by the National Science Foundation and the city's own resilience office, which adopted her shading standard in 2024.
